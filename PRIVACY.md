# Privacy Policy – FR Finder

_Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Author: GC Tech_

**FR Finder does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal data.** It has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no remote code.

## What the extension does
On `squig.link` pages only, it reads the public phone-book and measurement (`.txt`) files of the site you are visiting, computes similarities locally in your browser, and shows the result in a floating window.

## Data stored on your device
- **IndexedDB**: the downloaded measurement curves (numbers only), kept up to 30 days to avoid downloading them again.
- **localStorage** (of the squig.link page): window position, minimized state, selected focus mode, and the date of the last indexing.

This data never leaves your browser. You can remove it by clearing site data for the squig.link site, or by uninstalling the extension.

## Network requests
The only requests made are to the **same squig.link site you are visiting**, for its public data files, and only after you press "Index this database". No request is sent to the author or to any third party.

## Permissions
The extension requests no browser permissions. It runs only on `https://squig.link/*` and `https://*.squig.link/*` through content scripts.

## Children
The extension is not directed to children and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date above will be updated in this file.
